Microphone2Speaker

This is a fork. Microphone2Speaker is a modified version of the original “Microphone” app by Gaz Davidson (gaz@bitplane.net). The changes in this fork were made by Martin Viereck and are not endorsed by or affiliated with the original author.

The app sends the sound from your microphone straight to the phone's audio output. Use headphones to avoid feedback. It is handy as a live microphone monitor, a simple hearing aid, or for using your phone as a microphone connected to another device.

What is different in this fork: the main goal was to reduce the latency between speaking and hearing. The app now selects the device's native sample rate and hardware buffer size automatically, uses a low-latency audio path, and adapts to different phones. On Bluetooth headphones a noticeable delay remains — that part is caused by Bluetooth itself and cannot be removed by the app.

The original app and its history remain credited below. For the original app see bitplane.net.

Changes:


2.0 (fork by Martin Viereck)
  • Forked as “Microphone2Speaker” with its own app id, so it installs alongside the original app.
  • Greatly reduced audio latency: uses the device's native sample rate (no resampling), a low-latency playback path and hardware-sized buffers.
  • Automatic self-tuning of sample rate and buffer size for different devices, with safe fallbacks.
  • New app icon (microphone with sound waves) and a version/build stamp on the main screen.
